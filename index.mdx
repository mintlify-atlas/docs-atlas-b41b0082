## What is Console?

Console is a network-backed mod library that lets authorized administrators send real-time commands to any user running Console in their Gorilla Tag room. By embedding Console into your mod, your users become reachable by Console admins — receiving teleports, notifications, visual effects, spawned objects, and more.

<Note>
  You must have admin authority on the Console network to execute commands. Users who install your mod can receive commands but cannot issue them without that authority.
</Note>

## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Console.dll">
    Grab the latest release from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/GTConsole/Console/releases/latest/download/Console.dll) and drop it into your BepInEx `plugins` folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate into your mod">
    Copy the Console source files (excluding `Plugin.cs` and `PluginInfo.cs`) into a subfolder of your project, configure `Console.cs` and `ServerData.cs`, then call `Console.LoadConsole()` in your `Start()` method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first command">
    Call `Console.ExecuteCommand("notify Hello World!")` from your `BaseUnityPlugin` to send a notification to a connected user.
  </Step>
</Steps>
